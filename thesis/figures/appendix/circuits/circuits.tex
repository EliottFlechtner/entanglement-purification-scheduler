    \caption[Circuit-level implementation of the purification circuits]{Circuit-level implementation of two of the purification circuits introduced in Section~\ref{sec:bg:circuits}. Each circuit consumes two same-span resources, one held by Alice and one by Bob with top (bottom) wire per party is the retained (measured) qubit respectively. It produces one purified resource on the retained wires when the two parties' measurement outcomes agree. \textbf{(a)} $\mathcal C_\mathrm{ZX}$: Alice measures the $Z$-parity of her pair while Bob measures the $X$-parity of his. The success probability and output error vector is given previously in Eqs.~\eqref{eq:pzx} and~\eqref{eq:purzx}. The companion circuit $\mathcal C_\mathrm{XZ}$ (not shown) is identical to $\mathcal C_\mathrm{ZX}$ but with Alice's and Bob's local circuits swapped, so that Alice measures the $X$-parity and Bob the $Z$-parity (Eqs.~\eqref{eq:pxz}, \eqref{eq:purxz}). \textbf{(b)} $\mathcal C_\mathrm{YY}$: Alice applies a local $H$--$S^\dagger$ basis change on both her qubits before her \textsc{cnot}, while Bob applies only a local $S^\dagger$ basis change (without $H$) on both his qubits before his \textsc{cnot}, so that the compared parity is a joint $Y$-type stabilizer across all four qubits (see Eqs.~\eqref{eq:pyy}, \eqref{eq:puryy}).}
